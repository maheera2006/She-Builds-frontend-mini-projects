Student Registration & Result Portal

Follow these steps:
  1. Type your name, roll number and email.
  2. Choose your course.
  3. Check the subjects, then type the marks you scored in each.
  4. Click Calculate result.
Rule: you pass if you score at least 35% in every subject and 40% overall.

Subjects and marks

Subjects load when you choose a course. You can edit names and maximum marks, add or remove subjects.

Choose a course to load its subjects.
